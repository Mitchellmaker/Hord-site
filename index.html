02 / Let it make connections

Words and images.
More to find it by.

Hord uses text and visual information in supported saved content to make it easier to find again. Words in a screenshot. Recognisable subjects in a photo. Useful clues in a document.

Content understanding happens on your device using Apple technologies.

Enhanced text understanding depends on device eligibility and Apple Intelligence availability. Analysis varies with the content; semantic search currently uses an English model.

About search and processing

One visual library

Different things.
The same place.

Links, photos, screenshots, PDFs and documents can live together in Hord, alongside compatible audio, video and other file captures.

What can be saved depends on the content each app shares. Understanding and previews are available for supported content.

Across your devices

Saved on iPhone.
Found on iPad.

Hord uses your iCloud account and Apple’s private CloudKit database to synchronise supported saved content between iPhone and iPad.

Sync requires iCloud to be available. It can take time or pause, and some captures may not be eligible to sync.

Help with iCloud sync

Keep the connection

Back to
the original.

Eligible saved public links keep their source URL, so you can reopen the original when you want to go further.

External pages can change or become unavailable. Saving a link does not guarantee permanent access to its source.

Privacy, considered

Understood
on your device.

Hord uses Apple technologies for on-device content understanding. The current app has no identified advertising, third-party analytics or behavioural tracking.

Supported sync uses Apple’s private iCloud database. Public-link previews contact the relevant websites. No proprietary content-upload backend was identified in the app.
